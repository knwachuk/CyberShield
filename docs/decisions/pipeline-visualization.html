Abuse detection pipeline

Decomposed, calibrated, explainable. Each dimension of abuse is a separate detector that emits a calibrated score plus its evidence; results are fused into a severity tier; confident extremes are auto-handled and everything ambiguous goes to a person, whose decisions retrain the detectors.

6 · Route

Confident extreme → auto-handle (rare, conservative). Everything else → human.

Human loop Review queue→ reviewer decides (owns consent, sarcasm, counterspeech) Feedback store→ active-learning labels on the hardest cases ⤴ retrains the detectors
Release gate Fairness & calibration audit subgroup error + calibration checks must pass before any detector or fusion config ships ⤴

Colour shows how well software can do each job today: green detectors carry the verdict, amber detectors advise (and their errors are watched), and the two red detectors exist mainly to recognise their own uncertainty and route the case to a person rather than to decide it. The human loop and the fairness gate are cross-cutting: the loop feeds reviewer decisions back as training labels, and the gate blocks any release that fails subgroup fairness or calibration checks. See the ADRs in this directory for the reasoning behind each choice (ADR-0001 … ADR-0008).
